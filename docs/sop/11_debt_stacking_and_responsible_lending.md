# SOP 11 — Debt Stacking, Loan Cycling and Responsible Lending

**Synthetic bank procedure for the RM Assist POC.** The procedure illustrates responsible-credit controls and must be configured to the bank's approved policy.

## Definition
Debt stacking exists where a customer carries multiple concurrent loans, rapid credit enquiries or undisclosed obligations. Debt cycling exists where proceeds from a new loan are used primarily to service older loans, creating a self-reinforcing debt carousel.

This may be Ponzi-like in cash-flow mechanics, but it is not an investment Ponzi scheme unless separate evidence establishes such conduct.

## Investigation sequence
1. Reconcile the application declaration with bureau, Account Aggregator and bank-statement liabilities.
2. Calculate verified fixed obligations and FOIR using current income, not stated income alone.
3. Resolve disbursement-to-repayment loops within 48–72 hours.
4. Test whether a productive asset, inventory or business use is evidenced.
5. Identify hardship events, income compression, repeat intermediary fees and coached application behaviour.
6. Distinguish distress-driven borrowing from deliberate misrepresentation.

## Credit decision principle
Fresh cash-out credit must not be used to postpone an affordability failure. Where verified obligations already consume most monthly inflow, decline the cash-out application and route the customer to a stabilisation assessment.

## Customer treatment
Use non-accusatory language. Explain the affordability facts, stop repeated applications, and avoid presenting another high-cost loan as the default solution.
