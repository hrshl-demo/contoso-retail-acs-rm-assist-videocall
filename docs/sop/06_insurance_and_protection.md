# Insurance & Protection policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Ensure retail customers have adequate, suitable protection (term life, health, motor, home) as a needs-based cross-sell.

## Suitability
- Term cover broadly 10 to 15 times annual income; health cover sized to city and medical inflation.
- Disclose that insurance is a protection product, not an investment; no mis-selling and no forced bundling.

## Triggers
- Rising income (cover should track income); a new home loan (property and loan protection); a protection gap (no cover held).

## RM guidance
- Position an insurance top-up as a service-led conversation; share an indicative quote, never pressure.
- A suitability assessment and the free-look period apply; respect consent and DPDP.
