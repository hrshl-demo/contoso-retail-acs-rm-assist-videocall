# SOP 13 — Microfinance Exposure and Bureau Reconciliation

**Synthetic bank procedure for the RM Assist POC.**

## Objective
Build a complete liability view for customers with microfinance, digital-lender, NBFC and bank exposures before making a new credit decision.

## Minimum evidence
- latest credit-bureau report and enquiry history;
- consented Account Aggregator or bank-statement cash flows where available;
- lender statements, current outstanding and foreclosure values;
- repayment-frequency normalisation for weekly, fortnightly and monthly products;
- days-past-due and bounce history;
- loan-purpose evidence and end-use trail;
- intermediary or arrangement-fee payments.

## Reconciliation rules
- Treat undeclared but verified obligations as affordability inputs immediately.
- Do not double-count refinanced facilities; confirm actual closure.
- Convert all repayment frequencies into a monthly equivalent.
- Flag disbursements followed by older-lender repayments as potential debt-cycling links.
- Explain material bureau-versus-application contradictions to the authorised reviewer.

## RM output
The RM should receive a customer-safe explanation of the affordability gap, the evidence still required and the appropriate intervention lane. Restricted intermediary intelligence remains investigator-only.
