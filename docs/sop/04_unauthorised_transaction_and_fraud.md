# Unauthorised Transaction & Fraud policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Protect customers from fraud and apply the RBI customer-protection (limited liability) framework.

## Indicators
- Customer does not recognise a transaction; an unusual merchant or geography; multiple rapid e-commerce attempts.

## Actions
1. Block the instrument immediately.
2. Report to the fraud / risk unit; raise a dispute / chargeback (see the Card Dispute policy).
3. Educate the customer: never share OTP / CVV / PIN; the bank never asks for them.

## Limited liability (RBI)
- Zero liability: a third-party breach due to bank fault, or prompt reporting by the customer.
- Graded liability with delayed reporting.

## RM guidance
- Treat a reported unauthorised transaction as Critical / High priority; log a case and follow the SLA.
- Capture the time of the transaction, the last genuine transaction, and whether any OTP / credentials were shared.
