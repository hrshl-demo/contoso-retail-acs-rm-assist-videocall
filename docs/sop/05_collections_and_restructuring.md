# Collections & Restructuring policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Support customers who miss EMIs, while complying with the RBI Fair Practices Code and SMA/NPA classification norms.

## SMA classification (days past due)
- SMA-0: 1 to 30 dpd, SMA-1: 31 to 60 dpd, SMA-2: 61 to 90 dpd, then NPA.

## Options before any new credit
- Card EMI conversion / balance transfer to cut a high revolving APR.
- A one-time EMI deferral or step-down restructuring for genuine hardship.
- Re-aging only per policy and with documented hardship.

## Hard rule
- Do NOT offer a new loan or a higher limit to an account in collections or dispute. Stabilise first; protect the credit score.

## RM guidance (Fair Practices)
- Contact only within permitted hours; be respectful and non-coercive.
- Lead with relief that protects the customer CIBIL; explain the score impact of continued bounces.

## SMA-1 handling and legal boundary
- At SMA-1 (31 to 60 dpd) the priority is a restructuring conversation, not legal escalation.
- Offer a documented hardship restructuring (see Hardship Restructuring Playbook) before any demand notice.
- Do not threaten legal action, seizure or arrest at SMA-1; recovery suits belong to later stages and follow due process.

## Forbearance vs forgiveness
- Forbearance (step-down, deferral, tenure extension) is temporary relief: interest is deferred, not waived.
- Forgiveness (write-off of principal or accrued interest) is rare, needs senior approval, and is not a routine on-call concession.
- Always tell the customer that restructuring is temporary relief and interest is deferred, not forgiven.
