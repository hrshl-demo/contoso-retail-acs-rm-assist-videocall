# SOP 12 — Controlled Debt Consolidation and Financial Health Assist

**Synthetic bank procedure for the RM Assist POC.** A consolidation review is not an approval or entitlement.

## Two-lane intervention

### Lane A — Debt Reset Review (conditional product review)
A direct-pay consolidation may be considered only where all conditions are met:
- every lender and foreclosure amount is verified;
- proceeds are paid directly to lenders, with no customer cash-out;
- old facilities are closed or frozen as required;
- total monthly debt service falls by at least 25%;
- the customer retains a sustainable monthly surplus after essential expenses;
- no new borrowing is permitted during the stabilisation period;
- an authorised credit officer approves the structure.

### Lane B — Financial Health Assist (non-credit service)
Use when consolidation cannot restore affordability. The service may include:
- complete debt calendar and repayment prioritisation;
- hardship-budget review;
- lender-coordination support;
- due-date alignment requests where permitted;
- fraud/intermediary education;
- periodic RM check-ins;
- referral to approved independent debt-counselling channels where available.

## Product-positioning rule
The bank does not “sell another loan” to solve loan cycling. The product lane is conditional and subordinate to the responsible-lending decision. The non-credit lane is a valid customer outcome.
