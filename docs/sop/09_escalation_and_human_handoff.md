# Escalation & Human Handoff policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Define when an RM or the AI co-pilot must escalate or hand off to a specialist (Case-Dispute RM, fraud unit, Branch Manager).

## Escalate when
- An unauthorised transaction or suspected fraud is reported.
- An account is SMA-1 or worse, or the customer threatens closure (retention risk).
- A complaint cannot be resolved on the call, or it requires a fee waiver or a policy exception.

## Handoff rules
- The AI co-pilot NEVER approves credit, waives a charge, or commits an outcome; it logs a case and routes to the right human.
- Capture a clear summary, the commitment by the bank, and a follow-up date in the CRM case.

## RM guidance
- For a card-dispute customer, hand the chargeback to the Case-Dispute RM and the retention risk to the Branch Manager, with the case ids.
