# HNI Urgent Payment Service Recovery — Synthetic POC SOP

## Scope
This synthetic SOP governs urgent high-value payment holds where a customer reports medical or similarly time-sensitive distress. It applies to customer AM4827 in the POC and must not be treated as an actual bank policy.

## SR-01 — Acknowledge distress
During service hours, an urgent medical-payment concern should be acknowledged promptly. The RM should establish case ownership and a next-update commitment, without promising release or settlement.

## SR-02 — Minimum sufficient verification
Where beneficiary bank details changed recently, the minimum evidence pack is:

1. Revised invoice showing beneficiary identity and updated account details.
2. Signed beneficiary-change declaration.
3. Independent callback verification using the bank's approved customer contact record.
4. Completion of applicable screening and documentary consistency checks by Payments Operations.

Unrelated income or wealth documents should not be requested merely because the customer is HNI.

## SR-03 — Authority boundaries
The RM may acknowledge, coordinate and escalate. The Branch Manager may approve queue priority and management attention. Neither may release the payment, override screening, or promise a final completion time. Payments Operations remains the authoritative owner of verification and release-to-payment-queue status.

## SR-04 — Internal escalation standard
An escalation must state:

- Confirmed transaction and customer facts.
- Exact hold reason.
- Evidence already received.
- Missing control steps.
- Customer urgency and commitment deadline.
- The specific decision requested.
- A statement that no mandatory control override is requested.

## SR-05 — Audience separation
Internal records may contain control triggers, approval reasoning and operational ownership. Customer communications must contain empathy, confirmed facts, pending items, next action and next update time. Internal risk discussion or screening mechanics must not be copied into the customer message.

## SR-06 — Status semantics
"Priority review approved" does not mean "payment approved." "Verification complete and released to payment queue" does not mean beneficiary receipt or final settlement. Every communication must use the latest authoritative status and explain the distinction.

## SR-07 — Closure
Closure requires a chronology, decisions, evidence, internal approvals, customer communications, authoritative resolution and a preventive follow-up. The case should identify whether a service-recovery gesture is appropriate, but no gesture is automatic.
