# Card Dispute & Chargeback policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Handle disputed and unauthorised credit/debit card transactions fairly and within card-network (Visa/Mastercard/RuPay) and RBI timelines.

## Immediate steps for an unauthorised transaction
1. Hot-list / block the card to prevent further misuse.
2. Register the dispute and raise a chargeback with the card network.
3. Apply provisional credit where applicable, per network rules and RBI limited-liability guidance.
4. Share the reference/ticket id and the expected resolution SLA with the customer.

## Customer liability (RBI)
- Zero liability if the loss is due to bank negligence, or if reported promptly before further loss.
- Limited liability that scales with the delay in reporting; encourage immediate reporting.

## Timelines
- Acknowledge within 24 hours; resolve chargebacks typically within 7 to 90 days per network.

## RM guidance
- Service recovery comes BEFORE any sales conversation. Reassure, confirm the last genuine transaction, and log a case.
- Do not promise a specific outcome or a guaranteed refund; explain the chargeback process and the SLA.
