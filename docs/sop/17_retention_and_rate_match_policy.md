# Retention & Rate-Match policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Give the RM a controlled way to respond when a customer threatens to move the relationship or quotes a competitor's rate, without conceding uncommercial or off-policy terms.

## Golden rule
- No rate, limit, waiver or retention concession is ever committed on the call. Everything is indicative and confirmed in writing (see On-Call Offer Commitment policy).

## Rate-match authority (indicative bands)
- Relationship value high: up to ~1.5% discount, RM discretion.
- Relationship value medium: up to ~1% discount, Branch Manager approval.
- Relationship value low: up to ~0.5% discount, Credit Committee approval.
- At-risk / SMA account: no rate reduction bundled with new credit; restructure first.

## Competitor offers
- Do NOT rate-match an unverified verbal claim. Ask for the competitor's written offer letter (dated, on letterhead) before any material match is considered.
- Never disparage the competitor; compare on total value, not just headline rate.

## Non-rate retention levers (often better than a discount)
- Card EMI conversion to cut a high revolving APR to an installment rate.
- Late-fee or annual-fee waiver within RM authority.
- Restructuring / step-down for genuine hardship.
- Needs-based insurance or protection to close a gap.

## Prohibited
- Rate-match above the stated authority without escalation.
- A new limit or top-up plus a rate cut to an SMA / disputed account.
- Retroactive waiver of already-accrued interest (that is forgiveness, not retention).

## RM guidance
- Acknowledge the threat, protect the relationship, but stay inside policy: "I want to keep your business and I will put the best eligible offer to you in writing within 24 hours."
- Log the retention interaction and any competitor reference in CRM.
