# KYC / Re-KYC policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Establish and periodically refresh customer identity for individual (retail) customers, per the RBI Master Direction on KYC.

## When re-KYC is required
- Periodic refresh: Low risk every 10 years, Medium 8 years, High 2 years.
- On a change of address, name, or risk category; or when documents lapse.
- Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is acceptable for re-KYC.

## Documents
- Officially Valid Documents (OVD): PAN (mandatory for credit), Aadhaar (masked), passport, voter ID, driving licence.
- Income proof (salary slip / ITR / Form 16) for any loan or limit increase.

## RM guidance
- A pending video re-KYC BLOCKS any new credit (loan, card limit increase). Complete re-KYC first.
- Never read out a full PAN/Aadhaar; use masked values. Capture consent for re-KYC.
- For a customer flagged KYC due, offer to complete V-CIP on the call before discussing products.
