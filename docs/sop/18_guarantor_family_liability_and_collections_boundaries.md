# Guarantor, Family Liability & Collections Boundaries (Retail)

## Purpose
Answer, accurately and lawfully, when a customer asks whether family are liable for a debt or fears seizure and coercive recovery. Clarify nominee vs guarantor status and the RBI Fair Practices limits on collections.

## Nominee vs guarantor
- A nominee is a beneficiary designee only and carries NO liability for the debt during the customer's lifetime.
- A guarantor / co-applicant is liable only where a signed personal-guarantee deed or joint-application mandate exists.
- If no guarantee deed is on file, the family member is not liable, regardless of relationship.

## Family liability on unsecured facilities
- A personal loan or credit card is unsecured; the cardholder / borrower is the sole liable party.
- Spouse, children, parents and siblings have NO automatic liability unless explicitly named as a co-applicant / guarantor with a signed mandate.
- A nominee on a savings account is not a co-applicant on a credit facility.

## Seizure & asset recovery
- Unsecured loans / cards: no asset seizure. Recovery is via demand notice and, if unresolved, a civil recovery suit against the borrower only.
- Secured loans: enforcement follows the specific security agreement and due legal process; a spouse's separate assets are not attachable for the borrower's unsecured debt.
- Never threaten seizure of a home, business or family assets for an unsecured facility.

## Collections contact boundaries (RBI Fair Practices)
- Contact the borrower (and a signed guarantor, if any) only, on verified numbers, within permitted hours.
- Do NOT contact spouse, family, employer or neighbours to apply repayment pressure without the borrower's written consent. Employer contact is limited to employment verification, not repayment demand.
- No abusive language and no false threats of arrest, police action or seizure.

## Permitted vs prohibited language
- Permitted: "Your EMI is overdue; please pay by the due date or call us to discuss restructuring options."
- Prohibited: "Your family will be sued", "we will seize your house", "the police will arrest you", "we will call your employer and expose you".

## RM guidance
- Reassure with the facts: state clearly who is and is not liable, based on the record.
- If the customer alleges coercive or unauthorised contact, log a grievance and escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer (see Fair Practices & Grievance policy).
