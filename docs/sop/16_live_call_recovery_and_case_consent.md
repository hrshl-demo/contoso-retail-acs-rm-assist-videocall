# Live Call Recovery, Complaint Continuity and Case Consent

## Purpose

This policy governs AI-assisted customer conversations in Video Assist, especially card disputes, interest or fee complaints, repayment stress, attrition threats and service-recovery situations. The AI assists the Relationship Manager (RM); it does not register a formal complaint or service case merely because the customer asks a question, expresses anger or requests a waiver.

## Mandatory sequence before a new CRM case

1. **Acknowledge and clarify.** Identify whether the customer is asking for information, challenging a charge, reporting hardship, disputing a transaction or asking for a formal complaint.
2. **Check existing records first.** Search the CRM, dispute record, service request and task history. An open issue must be continued under its existing reference; duplicate cases are prohibited.
3. **Explain the source-backed position.** Quote only verified amounts, dates, status, contractual rate and available policy options. Do not invent a refund date, provisional credit, waiver or approval.
4. **Separate disputed and undisputed exposure.** For an interest complaint, first isolate charges potentially attributable to an open disputed amount. Then explain the verified undisputed balance and any contractual charges.
5. **Offer the standard remedy path.** This may include dispute-status confirmation, fee attribution review, chargeback or provisional-credit follow-up, EMI conversion, hardship assessment, restructuring or an authorised grievance escalation.
6. **Determine whether the issue remains unresolved.** A factual status inquiry, anger, threat to leave, request to remove all interest, or statement that the customer will not pay is not by itself proof that the standard route is exhausted.
7. **Ask explicit permission.** Only after the normal route has been explained and remains insufficient may the RM ask: “Would you like me to register a formal case so the bank can track this issue and follow up with you?”
8. **Wait for a later, clear customer response.** Only an explicit and voluntary yes to that specific question permits registration. Silence, frustration, agreement with facts, “do whatever you need,” a new question or an ambiguous response is not consent.
9. **Register under a named human.** The CRM write must record the customer’s confirming utterance, the turn on which consent was captured and the named RM who approved the write.

## When a new case must not be created

- The customer is asking for the status of an existing dispute or complaint.
- An open service request, chargeback, grievance, fee-review task or restructuring task already covers the issue.
- The customer asks for a blanket waiver before the disputed and undisputed components are explained.
- The customer threatens to leave, is angry or alleges unfair treatment, but the RM has not yet completed the recovery conversation.
- The customer has not explicitly consented to a formal case after being asked.
- The customer’s response is ambiguous or addresses a different issue.

## Data-backed retention response

For an attrition threat, the RM should avoid a generic promise to “look into it.” The response should identify the existing issue reference, relevant amount and current status; explain the next evidence or ownership check; and distinguish any disputed amount from the remaining verified exposure. The RM may offer a time-bound follow-up, but must not promise a refund, interest waiver, approval or settlement.

## Synthetic Rakesh Sharma example

The source record shows a card limit of ₹3,00,000, card outstanding of approximately ₹3,06,247.59 and an APR of 42%. A ₹48,500 GlobalMart Online transaction dated 19 February 2026 is already under dispute reference SR-B-202602-001. Isolating the disputed amount leaves an indicative undisputed balance of approximately ₹2,57,747.59, or about 85.9% of the limit. The conduct record also shows two bounced personal-loan EMIs (auto-debit returns), so the loan is flagged SMA-1 (early stress).

The policy-safe order is therefore:

- continue SR-B-202602-001 and obtain chargeback/provisional-credit status;
- check whether any interest or fees are attributable to the disputed leg;
- explain the remaining verified balance and contractual rate;
- assess eligible EMI conversion, hardship or restructuring options;
- only if the issue remains unresolved, ask the customer for permission to register a formal grievance or service case;
- register only after a later explicit yes.

## Authority boundary

The AI may classify intent, retrieve records, prepare a recommendation and draft a proposed case. It must not create the case before the consent gate. The RM owns the conversation, confirms that the policy route has been completed, asks for permission and remains the named human accountable for the CRM action.
