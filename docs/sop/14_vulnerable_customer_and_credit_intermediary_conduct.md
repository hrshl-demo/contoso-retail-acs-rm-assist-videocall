# SOP 14 — Vulnerable Customer and Credit-Intermediary Conduct

**Synthetic bank procedure for the RM Assist POC.**

## Vulnerability indicators
Medical shocks, income loss, bereavement, senior age, low digital literacy, repeated high-cost borrowing, coercion, coached applications and unexplained intermediary fees may require a customer-protection posture.

## Intermediary review
- identify repeated fees immediately after loan disbursement;
- identify common devices, contact details or application patterns across borrowers where legally and operationally permitted;
- preserve customer communications;
- do not disclose restricted network-intelligence sources to the customer or RM;
- escalate suspected misconduct to the approved conduct/fraud function.

## Customer communication
Explain the decision using verified affordability and disclosure facts. Do not accuse the customer of fraud solely because an agent was involved. Offer a clear route to submit missing lender data and a non-credit assistance path.
