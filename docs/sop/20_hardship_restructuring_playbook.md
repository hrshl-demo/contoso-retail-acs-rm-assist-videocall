# Hardship Restructuring Playbook (Retail)

## Purpose
Give the RM a consistent, fair and compliant way to respond when a customer reports income loss or cannot meet EMIs, especially at SMA-1.

## When it applies
- Missed / bounced EMIs, a drop in income, or a documented shock (medical, business downturn, job loss).
- Restructuring is offered before legal escalation for a genuine, documented hardship.

## Hardship categories & evidence
- Income loss: latest ITR / payslips and 3 months' bank statements showing the decline; for self-employed, P&L and GST trend.
- Medical shock: discharge summary / treatment plan and recent bills.
- Business downturn / seasonality: 6-12 months P&L, GST returns and statements.

## Restructuring options (indicative)
- Card EMI conversion: move a high-APR revolving balance to a fixed installment rate — permanent relief on card cost.
- Step-down: reduce the EMI (for example ~25%) for a fixed relief window, then resume.
- EMI deferral: a short interest-only period with catch-up afterwards.
- Tenure extension: lengthen the term to lower the EMI (raises total interest).
- Payment-date realignment: match the EMI date to the customer's cash-inflow date.

## Forbearance vs forgiveness (explain clearly)
- Forbearance = temporary relief; interest is deferred, not waived. The customer resumes normal payments later.
- Forgiveness = write-off of principal or accrued interest; rare, needs senior approval, not offered as a routine on-call concession.
- Always tell the customer restructuring is temporary relief, not a permanent rate cut, and interest is deferred, not forgiven.

## Guardrails
- No approval or exact terms on the call; assess and confirm in writing (see On-Call Offer Commitment policy).
- No new credit or higher limit to a stressed account; stabilise and protect the CIBIL first.
- Non-coercive, within permitted hours; explain the score impact of continued bounces without threats.

## RM guidance
- Lead with an affordability review, not collections language: "Let's understand what changed in your cash flow, then I'll review the relief options you're eligible for."
- Capture the hardship and the document checklist in CRM and set the follow-up date.
