# On-Call Offer Commitment Protocol (Retail)

## Purpose
Prevent verbal-only approvals and misunderstandings when a customer demands an on-the-spot yes, a rate, a limit, a waiver or a new facility "today".

## Principle
- Nothing is approved or committed on the call. Every offer is indicative and confirmed in writing within 24 hours after eligibility review.

## What the RM must NOT say
- "You're approved", "consider it done", "your new limit is live", "I'll transfer the funds tomorrow".
- Any figure the customer could reasonably treat as a binding sanction.

## Mandatory sequence
- Acknowledge and clarify the request on the call, without committing.
- Review eligibility after the call: KYC / re-KYC status, SMA status, FOIR, active dispute, security.
- Route for the correct approval authority; only then send a written offer.
- The written offer states product, rate, tenure, EMI, total cost, conditions and a validity window; funds move only after the customer accepts in writing and signs the sanction.

## Hard gates
- A pending video re-KYC blocks any new credit, limit increase or consolidation drawdown until completed.
- An account in collections / dispute is stabilised first; no new credit is committed.
- Valid OTP authentication is identity verification, not consent to a product or a transaction.

## RM guidance
- Use calm, firm language: "I can't approve this on the call, but I will review it properly and send you a written offer within 24 hours with exact numbers. You'll have time to accept in writing."
- Never re-quote new terms on a follow-up call to end pressure; refer the customer to the written offer already sent.
