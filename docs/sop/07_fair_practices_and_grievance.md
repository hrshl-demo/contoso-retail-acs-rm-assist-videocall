# Fair Practices & Grievance Redressal policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Treat customers fairly and resolve complaints within defined timelines (RBI Fair Practices Code and the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme).

## Principles
- Transparency on charges, rates and terms; no mis-selling; non-coercive collections.
- Accessible complaint channels; acknowledge and track every grievance.

## Grievance timelines
- Acknowledge promptly; resolve within about 30 days. Escalate unresolved cases to the Principal Nodal Officer / Banking Ombudsman.

## Collections conduct
- Contact the borrower (and a signed guarantor, if any) only, on verified numbers, within permitted hours.
- Do not contact spouse, family, employer or neighbours to apply repayment pressure without the borrower's written consent.
- No abusive language and no false threats of arrest, police action or asset seizure. See Guarantor, Family Liability & Collections Boundaries policy.

## RM guidance
- For a disputed fee or charge, log it for review; explain eligibility for a waiver honestly and do not promise one.
- Every complaint raised on a call should be logged as a CRM case with a clear next step and owner.
- If the customer alleges coercive or unauthorised contact, log a grievance and escalate to the Principal Nodal Officer.
