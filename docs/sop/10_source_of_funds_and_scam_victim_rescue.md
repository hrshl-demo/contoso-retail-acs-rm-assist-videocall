# SOP 10 — Source of Funds and Scam-Victim Rescue

**Synthetic bank procedure for the RM Assist POC.** This document is not legal advice and does not replace the bank's approved AML, fraud, KYC or law-enforcement procedures.

## Purpose
Separate legitimate life-event money movements from potentially manipulated or fraudulent payment legs, while protecting a customer who may be a scam victim.

## Required investigation sequence
1. Establish the customer's normal behavioural baseline.
2. Segment the money flow by source, destination, relationship and purpose.
3. Validate source-of-funds documents against payer, ownership and amount.
4. Correlate beneficiary novelty, device/session changes, authentication events and customer communications.
5. Test competing explanations: legitimate activity, scam inducement, account takeover, deliberate third-party movement and coercion.
6. Use independent registered-channel contact where manipulation is suspected.
7. Minimise customer document requests to the unresolved issue.

## Customer-protection controls
- Do not equate valid OTP authentication with informed customer consent.
- Treat remote-access guidance, guaranteed-return promises, secrecy instructions and payee-name mismatches as converging risk signals rather than standalone proof.
- Preserve communications and device telemetry where permitted.
- Attempt payment recall or beneficiary escalation promptly when a posted scam payment is identified.
- Consider narrowing a broad restriction once legitimate money-flow legs are verified, subject to authorised review.

## RM disclosure boundary
The RM may explain the high-level review category, required customer actions, ownership team and expected next stage. Exact detection thresholds, restricted intelligence, suspicious-transaction reporting and law-enforcement-sensitive information must not be disclosed.

## Outcome
The outcome is customer protection and service recovery—not a product sale. Product discussions must be separate, suitability-led and occur only after the rescue case is closed.
