# Loan Eligibility & FOIR policy (Retail)

## Purpose
Assess eligibility for personal loans, credit-card limit increases and home-loan top-ups for individual customers.

## Eligibility gates (all must hold for a pre-approved / limit-increase offer)
- CIBIL score 750 or above, with no recent serious delinquency.
- Stable or rising income; clean repayment record (no EMI/auto-debit bounces in the last 12 months; at most one minor).
- FOIR (Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio) after the new EMI at or below 50%.
- KYC valid (no pending re-KYC).

## Card limit increase
- Favour customers with LOW utilisation and high, regular spends. Do NOT increase the limit on a revolving / over-utilised card; offer EMI conversion instead.

## Home-loan top-up
- On-time existing home loan, healthy loan-to-value after the top-up, current valuation.

## RM guidance
- Never commit a rate or amount on the call; all offers are indicative and subject to final eligibility.
- For a blocked customer, decline gracefully and state exactly what to fix (re-KYC, clear bounces, reduce utilisation).
